Marketplaces publish what sellers ask. What something actually sold for is
either unpublished or scattered across sold-listing pages and founder posts,
so I collected the public closed-deal data into one table.

198 deals, $1,000 to $2B. Sources are marketplace exit prices, broker
closed-deal records, and founder disclosures I read individually.

Two things I did not expect:

- Small products clear at high multiples, large ones do not. Under $10k the
  median cleared at ~8x annual revenue; in the $100k-$1M band it is ~0.9x.
  Price appears to have a floor near $1,000 regardless of revenue.

- Seven products earning under $600/year still sold: $1,000, $1,000, $1,000,
  $1,050, $2,000, $5,000, $5,000.

There is a valuation page too, which shows the nearest real deals by revenue
instead of applying a formula: https://kwonhyundong.github.io/sold-for/worth/

Limits, up front: the under-$10k band has only 10 deals with revenue
attached. Broker deals skew the set toward $100k+. Comparables mix SaaS,
content sites and ecommerce, which clear at very different multiples -- hence
listing the individual deals rather than just a range.

No signup, no ads, no tracking. It re-collects daily. Happy to add any
closed-deal source I have missed.
