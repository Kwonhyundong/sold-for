LONG DESCRIPTION  (약 600자)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Every marketplace shows asking prices. What a software business actually sold
for is either unpublished or scattered across sold-listing pages, broker
archives and founder posts.

Sold For collects those closed deals into one searchable table: 198 so far,
ranging from $1,000 weekend projects to a $2B acquisition, filterable by deal
size and by source. Every row links to where the figure came from.

The data shows things asking prices hide. Products under $10k clear at a
median of 8x annual revenue while $100k-$1M businesses clear at 0.9x, because
price has a floor near $1,000 regardless of revenue. Web products clear far
better than mobile apps at the same revenue.

A companion valuation page takes your annual revenue and shows the closest
real deals rather than applying a formula, so you can judge the comparison
yourself.

Free, no signup, no ads, no tracking. Re-collects and republishes daily.
