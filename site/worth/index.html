Small products sell at high multiples; bigger ones do not. Prices have a floor around $1,000 whether a product earns anything or not, so a tiny product clears at many times its revenue, while a mid-sized business clears near one year of it.

How the estimate is made. No formula. It finds the closed deals whose revenue is closest to yours and shows what those actually sold for, along with the deals themselves, so you can judge the comparison yourself. A formula picks a band and can be quietly wrong about which one; comparables cannot be.

What this cannot tell you. Comparable deals mix SaaS, content sites, apps and ecommerce, and those sell at different multiples of the same revenue. Margin matters more than revenue and is rarely published. That is why the deals themselves are listed: judge the comparison, do not just take the range.

Where the numbers come from. Only closed deals — marketplace exit prices, broker closed-deal records, and founder disclosures read one by one. Asking prices are excluded on purpose: listings at 28× and 31× revenue are common, and including them would corrupt the benchmark. Bands marked thin sample have too few deals with revenue attached to be relied on, and are shown rather than hidden.
