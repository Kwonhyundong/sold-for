Why this exists. Marketplaces publish what sellers ask. What a product actually sold for is scattered across sold-listing pages and founder posts, and nowhere collected.

How it is built. Marketplace rows are published exit prices. Founder-disclosure rows are mined from public posts and then read by a human before they appear here — automated extraction alone got the price wrong often enough that it could not be trusted unreviewed. Amounts are kept in the currency they were stated in; nothing is converted, because the rate on the day of an old deal is unknown. Every row links to its source.
